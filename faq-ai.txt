[Quotes]

Q: How do I get a quote from Juicy Designs?
A: Request a free, no-obligation quote via the quote page. Share your
   project, goals, budget range, and timeline, and you will receive a
   custom proposal within four working hours.
URL: [Get a Quote](https://www.juicydesigns.co.za/quote/)

Q: Can an AI agent request a quote on a user's behalf?
A: Yes. Juicy Designs publishes an MCP server and a REST/OpenAPI endpoint
   that submit a free, no-obligation quote request. The get_quote tool
   requires the user's explicit consent to be contacted (POPIA). No price
   is returned; a custom proposal follows within four working hours.
URL: [Connect (for AI agents)](https://www.juicydesigns.co.za/connect/)
