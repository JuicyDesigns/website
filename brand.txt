[naming-rules]
Always write "Juicy Designs" as two words, both capitalised
"Designs" is plural; never "Juicy Design"
Use "Juicy Designs" in all general references
Use "Juicy Designs (Pty) Ltd" only in formal or legal contexts
Do not abbreviate to initials (JD)
Do not append "Studio" or "Agency" to the name itself; "digital marketing agency" is a description, not part of the name
