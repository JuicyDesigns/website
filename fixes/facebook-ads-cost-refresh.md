## 2. FAQ section (add as visible on-page content near the end)

**How much do Facebook ads cost in South Africa?**
Most South African businesses spend R6,000 or more per month on Facebook ad
spend, plus an agency management fee if the campaigns are managed. CPMs
typically run R30 to R90.

**What is a good cost per lead for Facebook ads in South Africa?**
Cost per lead typically ranges from R40 to R250 depending on the industry,
offer, and targeting quality. Service businesses with strong offers sit at the
lower end; competitive financial and insurance niches at the upper end.

**What budget should I start with for Facebook ads?**
A starting budget of R6,000 per month in ad spend is a practical minimum for
enough data to optimise against. Smaller budgets can work for tightly
geo-targeted local campaigns.

**Are Facebook ads worth it for small businesses in South Africa?**
Yes, when the offer and landing page are strong. Facebook's targeting reaches
buyers earlier in the funnel than Google Search, which makes it effective for
awareness and lead generation at lower CPMs than search ads.
